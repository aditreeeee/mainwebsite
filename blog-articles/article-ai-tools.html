Introducing eGlobe AI Tools: your Sales Agent, Smartdesk & Admin Agent.

Three AI agents now sit inside every eGlobe property, working across WhatsApp, your front desk and your PMS.

Quick answer

Three AI agents now sit inside every eGlobe property: one that converts guest enquiries into bookings 24/7, one that assists your front desk at check-in, and one that answers business questions like "what was my occupancy last week?" instantly.

Our mission is simple: help hoteliers spend less time switching between systems and more time running a great property. Today, we're launching eGlobe AI Tools, built directly into your existing PMS, Channel Manager and POS screens.

Want to see it live? Book a 20-minute walkthrough with our team.

What's inside eGlobe AI Tools

Three agents, each built for a specific job your team already does every day:

  1. AI Sales Agent, responds instantly to WhatsApp, website and social enquiries, recommends rooms and pricing, and pushes confirmed bookings straight into your PMS. Works 24/7, without a human in the loop.
  2. AI Smartdesk, assists your front desk staff during check-in and check-out, answers common guest questions, and retrieves reservation details from your PMS in seconds.
  3. AI Admin Agent, answers business questions in plain language, "What was my occupancy last week?", "How did Booking.com perform this month?", pulling straight from your live PMS and Channel Manager data.

Built in, not bolted on

Unlike a standalone chatbot you have to configure and maintain separately, eGlobe AI Tools reads and writes to the same PMS, Channel Manager and POS data your team already works from. There's no second dashboard to check and no data to reconcile.

Rolling out now

eGlobe AI Tools is available today across all eGlobe properties. If you're already on eGlobe, reach out to our support team to get it enabled. If you're evaluating eGlobe for the first time, our sales team can show you AI Tools alongside the rest of the platform in a single walkthrough.

Questions about AI Tools? Talk to our team, or explore the full product ecosystem.
