How It Works

What Kitchen Order Ticket (KOT) is, and how it works.

KOT routes orders from POS straight to a kitchen display or printer the instant they're placed, so the kitchen gets accurate tickets in real time instead of relying on handwritten or shouted orders.

1

Order is entered in POS

At the table, counter, or for takeaway/delivery.

2

It routes to the kitchen instantly

The ticket appears on a kitchen display or prints automatically.

3

Kitchen marks progress

Items are tracked as preparing or ready, visible back at the outlet.

4

Nothing gets lost in translation

No re-entry, no misheard orders, everything traces back to the original ticket.
